    What you can say
    The word
    Say anything and it becomes the word. Start with "word" to force it: "word gold".
    Material
    Say its name: "gold", "glass", "kraft board", "wax". Or "next material".
    Colour
    "blue", "red", "lime". Or "next colour".
    Font
    "font Anton", "font Shrikhand". Or "next font".
    Look
    "mirror", "kaleidoscope", "trail", "print", "plain".
    Tool
    "pull", "twist", "smear", "poke", "magnet", "move".
    Moves
    "melt", "wobble", "set", "shatter", "explode", "bigger", "smaller", "reset".
    Layers
    "new layer", "next layer", "previous layer", "delete layer".
    Background
    "background off". To add a photo, use Background → Your picture.
    Save
    "save" for a picture. "action" starts a clip, "cut" stops it.
    Your voice
    Louder makes the word bigger. A low voice squashes it, a high voice stretches it, a steady hum makes it ripple.
    Type a word. Then pull it.

    TypeType a word in "Your word" on the right. Or turn on Voice and say it.

    PullDrag the word with the mouse. Or press Start camera and pinch it with your thumb and first finger; two hands stretch it. Hold a fist to reset.

    ChangeUse the panels on the right: font, material, colour, size, background. With the camera on, a right fist opens big tiles next to your hand.

    SpeakTurn on Voice and say a word, or a command like "gold" or "next font". See "What you can say".

    StackNew layer keeps your word and starts another on top.

    SaveSave PNG or Record clip. Press E or say "explode" to blow it all away and start again.

    Your camera and mic stay on this computer.

    How to use your hands

    Pinchpinch the word with your thumb and first finger and pull. Either hand works; two hands stretch it.

    Right fisthold for half a second to open big tiles next to your hand. Point at a tile with your right hand and pinch to choose it. Fist again to close.

    Left fisthold to reset the word.

    The restthe panels on the right are for the mouse; your hands stay on the canvas.
